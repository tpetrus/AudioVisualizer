of each file can be checked. They are imported with `?url`, so the build emits them hashed under `/assets/` with
immutable caching.

## MakeHumanDancer.glb

- Source: the MakeHuman community project, https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman at commit
  `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`, files under `makehuman/data/`:
  - `3dobjs/base.obj` (base mesh), sha256 `8e761e6624b8f54536409135d1636da63b32486a90d4897f84e121d144f6fb4c`
  - `rigs/default.mhskel` (skeleton), sha256 `99f179bce0aa850b45d4191a1d0d234c5851f881c057439470ded3bddf729a24`
  - `rigs/default_weights.mhw` (skin weights), sha256 `0f3641d651ae3d00ad6b4ccee43142edb109d3bd909d27d9e4139ef1beed8625`
  - `skins/default.mhmat` (default skin material, read and hash-checked only; it carries no diffuse color),
    sha256 `3df4dece5e9293f36a02a2e1fdb6d0f6f33d31584abc9219937b1cbbd8d9a377`
- Authors and copyright holders: Data Collection AB (https://www.datacollection.se), Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier
  (copyright 2020 for the mesh and material, 2020/2021 for the rig and weights)
- License: CC0 1.0. The repository's LICENSE.md section C places the base mesh, proxies, targets, textures and the other
  assets under CC0 1.0 (LICENSE.ASSETS.md), and section D says MakeHuman claims nothing over exports or script-generated
  output. `base.obj` states it was explicitly released as CC0 in September 2020, and the `.mhskel` and `.mhw`
  files carry `"license": "CC0"`.
- Changes: generated by `scripts/dancing-character-model/build-model.ts`. Only the `body` group of the base mesh is kept
  (quads split into triangles, reindexed); the helper and joint groups are dropped, but the joint vertices are
  still used to place the bones (each joint is the centroid of its
  vertices). Positions are scaled from decimeters to meters.
  Smooth normals are generated, there are no UVs or textures, and the material is one `Skin` (base color sRGB #c68a6d,
  roughness 0.6, metallic 0; the source `.mhmat` has no diffuse color). All 163 bones keep their MakeHuman names. Bone
  rest frames follow the Blender convention (local +Y from head to tail, roll from the bone's rotation plane). Skin
  weights keep the four strongest influences per vertex, renormalized to sum 1. There is no animation.
- Regenerate (from the repository root, `<input-dir>` holds the four files above):
  `npx esbuild scripts/dancing-character-model/build-model.ts --bundle --platform=node --format=esm --packages=external
  --log-level=warning --outfile=node_modules/.cache/build-model.mjs
  && node node_modules/.cache/build-model.mjs <input-dir>`
  (the output is byte-identical on every run; the script also loads the result with GLTFLoader and prints a rig report)
- Size: 794,820 bytes
- sha256: `5ab23b10fccd363828c591289843f99ab080f6a9fa99d008d43bf8dc42e3b5ff`
- Contents: 13,380 vertices, 26,756 triangles, 163 bones (single skin, 4 influences per vertex)
- Rest pose: MakeHuman's default A-pose (arms about 43 degrees out from straight down, elbows slightly bent), facing +Z,
  the figure's left (`.L`) side at +X, about 1.67 m tall; the origin is near the pelvis, so the feet are near y = -0.82
