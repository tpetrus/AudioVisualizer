# Dancing character assets

MakeHumanDancer.glb is CC0 1.0 (body and outfit alike) (public domain dedication): no attribution is required, but its
source is recorded here so it can be checked. MakeHumanDances.glb is built from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture
Database and is under the CMU terms recorded below, not CC0. Both are imported with `?url`, so the build emits them
hashed under `/assets/` with immutable caching.

## MakeHumanDancer.glb

- Source: the MakeHuman community project, https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman at commit
  `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`, files under `makehuman/data/`:
  - `3dobjs/base.obj` (base mesh), sha256 `8e761e6624b8f54536409135d1636da63b32486a90d4897f84e121d144f6fb4c`
  - `rigs/default.mhskel` (skeleton), sha256 `99f179bce0aa850b45d4191a1d0d234c5851f881c057439470ded3bddf729a24`
  - `rigs/default_weights.mhw` (skin weights), sha256 `0f3641d651ae3d00ad6b4ccee43142edb109d3bd909d27d9e4139ef1beed8625`
  - `skins/default.mhmat` (default skin material, read and hash-checked only; it carries no diffuse color),
    sha256 `3df4dece5e9293f36a02a2e1fdb6d0f6f33d31584abc9219937b1cbbd8d9a377`
- Authors and copyright holders: Data Collection AB (https://www.datacollection.se), Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier
  (copyright 2020 for the mesh and material, 2020/2021 for the rig and weights)
- License: CC0 1.0. The repository's LICENSE.md section C places the base mesh, proxies, targets, textures and the other
  assets under CC0 1.0 (LICENSE.ASSETS.md), and section D says MakeHuman claims nothing over exports or script-generated
  output. `base.obj` states it was explicitly released as CC0 in September 2020, and the `.mhskel` and `.mhw`
  files carry `"license": "CC0"`.
- Changes: generated by `scripts/dancing-character-model/build-model.ts`. Only the `body` group of the base mesh is kept
  (quads split into triangles, reindexed); the helper and joint groups are dropped, but the joint vertices are
  still used to place the bones (each joint is the centroid of its
  vertices). Positions are scaled from decimeters to meters.
  Smooth normals are generated, there are no UVs or textures, and the material is one `Skin` (base color sRGB #c68a6d,
  roughness 0.6, metallic 0; the source `.mhmat` has no diffuse color). All 163 bones keep their MakeHuman names. Bone
  rest frames follow the Blender convention (local +Y from head to tail, roll from the bone's rotation plane). Skin
  weights keep the four strongest influences per vertex, renormalized to sum 1. There is no animation.
- Regenerate (from the repository root, `<input-dir>` holds the four files above and `<outfit-dir>` the outfit files
  listed under "Outfit" below):
  `npx esbuild scripts/dancing-character-model/build-model.ts --bundle --platform=node --format=esm --packages=external
  --log-level=warning --outfile=node_modules/.cache/build-model.mjs
  && node node_modules/.cache/build-model.mjs <input-dir> --outfit <outfit-dir>`
  (the output is byte-identical on every run with the same sharp/libvips build; the script also loads the result with
  GLTFLoader, validates it and prints a rig report)
- Size: 1,262,280 bytes
- sha256: `a626b4237897949af3dae59ceffacde9d04657617bb999c4643b0d008e37baf7`
- Contents: 16,446 vertices, 30,680 triangles, 163 bones; five skinned meshes sharing one skeleton (4 influences per
  vertex): `Body` 8,510 vertices / 16,932 triangles (4,912 body faces hidden under the clothes are removed), `Outfit`
  4,035 / 7,168, `Shoes` 1,744 / 3,064, `Hair` 2,061 / 3,344, `Eyes` 96 / 172
- Rest pose: MakeHuman's default A-pose (arms about 43 degrees out from straight down, elbows slightly bent), facing +Z,
  the figure's left (`.L`) side at +X, about 1.67 m tall; the origin is near the pelvis, so the feet are near y = -0.82

  - `60.asf` (subject 60 skeleton), http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/60/60.asf, sha256

  - `60_01.amc`, http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/60/60_01.amc, sha256
    `d06e823aeb985cfa9382ac54c09a0ed4cd716e34e68a90f535065974dfc7201f`
  - `60_02.amc`, http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/60/60_02.amc, sha256
    `6a7b6b0d0a420cce041bda648255082370aad72185917a678af9c1bcc0929199`
  - `15.asf` (subject 15 skeleton), http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/15/15.asf, sha256
    `70693bac5f31e3b0892c398fb08b52da7b4c00003f55ce57e36ab58e63d93960`
  - `15_04.amc`, http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/15/15_04.amc, sha256
    `b32fa0654dce9db467aecfdd3bb730f33e6cc0c60b3ac5aa14da90ed576ca05e`
- Trials (CMU descriptions): 55_02 "lambada dance"; 60_01 and 60_02 "salsa dance"; 15_04 "wash windows, paint; hand
  signals; dance - Egyptian walk, the Dive, the Twist, the Cabbage Patch; boxing".
- Source frames per dance (frames of the AMC file, 120 per second for subjects 55 and 15, 60 per second for subject 60):
  - lambada: 55_02 frames 1680-2116
  - twist: 15_04 frames 16204-16580
  - cabbage-patch: 15_04 frames 14160-14460
  - salsa: legs and hips from 60_02 frames 254-366, upper body from 60_01 frames 1976-2098
- License: "The motion capture data may be copied, modified, or redistributed without permission."
  (http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/faqs.php, read 2026-11-11)
- Acknowledgment: "The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with
  funding from NSF EIA-0196217."
- Changes: each stretch is retargeted to the MakeHuman rig of MakeHumanDancer.glb, its root travel is removed, its
  loop is warped to half a second per beat and closed, the hips are locked to the lowest foot (twist, cabbage-patch and
  salsa), and the arms are pushed clear of the torso. The twist's arm and spine movement is scaled down and its head
  turned forward. The salsa's upper body comes from 60_01, with the right arm mirrored from the left two beats later.
- Rebuild: `node node_modules/.cache/fetch-dances.mjs` and `node node_modules/.cache/build-dances.mjs`, bundled as
  described in the header comments of `scripts/dancing-character-dances/fetch.ts` and `build.ts`. The output is
  deterministic.
- Result: 196968 bytes, sha256 `cfed5c9bce3dc7ae76d2776ca2cb7577f89acfc0f5f6dbbe2df3cd53564f1c23`. Clips (30 keys per
  second, closed loops): lambada 8 beats (4 s, 121 keys), twist 8 beats (4 s, 121 keys), cabbage-patch 4 beats (2 s,
  61 keys), salsa 4 beats (2 s, 61 keys).
