How do I set up Stereo Mix?

Stereo Mix is a Windows recording device that carries everything your computer is playing.

  1. Right-click the speaker icon in the taskbar and choose Sound settings, then More sound settings (under Advanced).
  2. Open the Recording tab.
  3. Right-click an empty area and tick Show Disabled Devices.
  4. Right-click Stereo Mix and choose Enable.
  5. Come back here, click Choose device, and pick Stereo Mix.

No Stereo Mix, or it stays silent? Stereo Mix can't capture audio from a device that is its own sound device (for example USB, HDMI or Bluetooth headsets and DACs), and many drivers hide it entirely. The most reliable workaround is Voicemeeter Banana (free, from vb-audio.com), which acts as a virtual mixer:

  1. Install Voicemeeter Banana and restart your computer.
  2. Open Voicemeeter. At the top right, click A1 and choose your real speakers or headset (use the WDM or MME entry).
  3. In Windows Sound settings, set your default playback device to Voicemeeter Input.
  4. In Voicemeeter, make sure A1 is lit on the Voicemeeter VAIO strip, and also light B1. A1 sends sound to your headset, B1 sends it to this app.
  5. Back here, click Choose device and pick Voicemeeter Out B1 (or Voicemeeter Output).

Alternatives:

  • A simpler virtual cable such as VB-Cable: set CABLE Input as your Windows playback device, then pick CABLE Output here. To still hear the audio, open CABLE Output's properties in the Recording tab, go to Listen, and tick Listen to this device with your speakers selected.
  • The screen/tab audio option, which needs no setup.
